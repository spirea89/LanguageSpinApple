Privacy policy

Your practice stays yours.

Language Roulette does not collect or transmit personal data from the app to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Effective date: September 21, 2026
Developer: Mihai Spirea
Contact: supportlanguagelearning@gmail.com

Information on your device

Player names, scores, and game progress are used locally during play. The app stores your interface-language preference on your device. These details are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes its locally stored settings.

Speech

The app uses Apple’s speech synthesis with the installed German voice Anna. It does not record your voice or access your microphone. The app does not send question text to a developer-operated speech service. Voice downloads and Apple system services are provided by Apple.

No tracking or data sales

We do not track users across apps or websites, create advertising profiles, sell personal data, or integrate third-party advertising or analytics services in the app.

If you contact support

Email is optional and separate from gameplay. If you email supportlanguagelearning@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve the issue, not for advertising. Our mailbox is hosted by Google. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up, or as required by law. You can request deletion by contacting the same address.

This website and external links

These support pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by us, or contact forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under its privacy statement. Opening external links or sending email is subject to the relevant provider’s privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children or other players. A parent or guardian can contact us about a support message containing a child’s personal information.

Changes and questions

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you sent to support, email supportlanguagelearning@gmail.com.
