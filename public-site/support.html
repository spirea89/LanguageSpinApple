Help & support

Let’s get you playing.

Language Roulette is a German speaking practice game for iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Email us with your question or a description of the problem. Your iOS version and device model can help us troubleshoot. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

supportlanguagelearning@gmail.com

How to play

  1. Choose the players and number of rounds.
  2. Select the categories you want to practice.
  3. Start the game and spin the wheel.
  4. Answer the German question. Replay it or reveal an example answer if you need help.
  5. Award points to move to the next player. The game ends when everyone has completed their rounds.

How do I hear Anna’s voice?

The app uses only the German voice Anna, choosing the best installed quality automatically. In Settings, open Accessibility → Read & Speak (called Spoken Content on some iOS versions) → Voices → German. Download Anna, preferably an enhanced or premium version if offered. Return to the app and replay the question.

If Anna is unavailable, you can continue playing with the question text. Voice availability depends on your device and iOS version.

There is no sound

Check your media volume and whether sound is going to Bluetooth headphones or another output. Confirm Anna’s download has finished, then reopen the app and try replaying a question.

Languages and questions

The interface supports English and German; practice questions are in German. Use the globe button to change the interface language. Public releases include a curated question pack. Send content corrections or suggestions to our support address.

Privacy

The app does not send us your gameplay or settings. If you email us, we receive your email and the information you choose to include. Read the privacy policy.
